Document: D2C Return Policy
Version: 1.0

1. Return Window
Customers may request a return within 7 days
of receiving an eligible product.

2. Product Condition
Products must be unused and returned with
their original packaging.

3. Refund Processing
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7
business days after the returned product
passes inspection.

4. Non-Returnable Products
Products marked as non-returnable are not
eligible for standard returns.

5. Investigation Guidance
When return rates increase, investigate
product category, delivery delays, product
condition complaints, and customer feedback.
This guidance does not establish the cause
of any individual return.
